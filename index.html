Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions? Let's make it simple.

Here are some common questions businesses ask when looking for commercial cleaning services.

What types of businesses do you clean?

24C provides commercial cleaning solutions for offices, business properties, contractors, property managers and restoration companies.

Do you provide recurring cleaning?

Cleaning schedules can be structured around the needs and operations of your business. Contact us to discuss the frequency that works for your property.

Do you provide post-construction cleanup?

Yes. Post-construction cleaning is one of the core services, designed to help prepare newly completed spaces for owners, tenants and occupants.

Can cleaning be scheduled outside business hours?

Scheduling can be discussed based on your property, operating hours and cleaning requirements.

How do I request a quote?

Use the request-a-quote button on the website and provide your property details. The 24C team can then discuss your cleaning requirements and next steps.
